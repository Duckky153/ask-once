Support inbox drafter

Ask Once

Two weeks of support email for a legal-transcription app (called "the app" here). Each email goes into one lane. If one help page clearly answers it, a reply is drafted for a person to check and send. Anything time-critical, about money, confidential, about a filed record, or from an upset customer is held for a person. Questions that several accounts keep asking with no help page are flagged. The decisions come from short, fixed rules, not a live model, so each one can be explained in a sentence.

          Assumptions

          • The phrase lists, the thresholds and the hold categories are guesses. Real inbox history would replace them.
          • A help page drafts a reply only when it matches at least 2 of its phrases and beats the next page by at least 1. Ties and weak matches go to a person.
          • A question counts as a repeat once 3 different accounts ask it. One account asking several times is one person waiting, not a missing page.
          • "Call now" fires for a proceeding that is live, starts within 60 minutes, or is this morning with something failing. It assumes the account has a phone number on file and someone is free to call.
          • Matching is plain phrase matching on the subject and body, as whole words. Typos and unusual wording are missed, and those emails fall to a person, which is the safe direction.
          • "Cancel" anywhere holds an email for billing, even "cancel the live link". That's on purpose: a wrong hold costs a person a minute, a wrong automated answer about money costs trust.
          • Watched questions are phrase lists someone sets up after noticing a pattern. The page doesn't discover new topics on its own.
          • The 60 emails, 20 accounts, 8 help pages and the hand labels were written for this demo with AI tools. Times are shown as written, with no time zones.
